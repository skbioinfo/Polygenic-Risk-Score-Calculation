# Polygenic Risk Score (PRS) Analysis for Psoriasis Clinical Phenotypes
This repository contains the code and data used in the study:
### A partitioned polygenic risk score reveals distinct contributions to psoriasis clinical phenotypes across a multi-ethnic cohort (Journal of Translational Medicine, 2024). 
📌 Study Overview
Psoriasis is a chronic immune-mediated inflammatory skin disease with a polygenic genetic architecture. Prior research has identified many susceptibility loci, but the relationship between polygenic risk scores (PRS) and clinical phenotypes—especially across different ethnicities—remains poorly understood. 
### In this study, we used a multi-racial cohort of 607 psoriasis patients, genotyped with a genome-wide SNP array, to examine the association between genetic risk and clinical features including:
	•	Age of onset
	•	Psoriatic arthritis
	•	Body locations of lesions
	•	Psoriasis subtype
	•	Environmental triggers
	•	Comorbidities
	•	Therapeutic response 
### PRS were calculated for three sets of loci:
	•	PRS-ALL: All 88 psoriasis-associated SNPs
	•	PRS-HLA: 11 SNPs within the HLA region
	•	PRS-NoHLA: 77 SNPs outside the HLA region 
Analyses showed that HLA and non-HLA genetic contributions differ across phenotypes, and in some cases associations varied by ethnicity.
